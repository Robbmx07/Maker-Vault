Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no trial, no account and nothing to pay for.

Do I need to keep my original files?

Jigbook stores its own full copy of every file you add, so it doesn't depend on your originals. Once you've downloaded a backup, you can tidy up the loose copies. Downloads always give you the exact original file back.

Where are my files stored?

Inside your web browser's storage on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server. Use Settings → Download backup to keep a copy somewhere safe, and Restore to bring it back — even on another computer. Note that the copy you open on this website and a copy you download and open from your own computer are separate vaults; use backup and restore to move between them.

Does it need the internet?

No. It's a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser. Download it once and it works offline.

Does it work with Cricut?

For your design files, yes. Cricut Design Space keeps your projects in Cricut's cloud, and Jigbook can't connect to it or read those projects. But if you cut from SVG files (downloaded, bought, or made in software like Inkscape), drop those into Jigbook to keep them together with your material notes, settings and results.

Will it read my slicer's settings?

Best with PrusaSlicer: settings are read reliably from its G-code and 3MF files. It also recognises OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio and Cura output and LightBurn project files, but those are newer and less tested, so results may vary. SVG and STL details (size, layers) are read too. If a file's settings aren't recognised, the file is still stored and you can add the settings by hand.

How do I move to a new computer?

Download a backup on the old one, open Jigbook on the new one, and choose Restore from backup.
